TONALPOHUALLI

A 260-day count formed by interlocking 13 numbers and 20 day signs. The estimate advances from the historical 1 Cóatl anchor, accounting for Tena’s pre-conquest intercalary day.

13 × 20 = 260 days

365-DAY SOLAR CYCLE
— — CR-01 ESTIMATE
XIUHPOHUALLI

A 365-day year: 18 veintenas of 20 days, followed by 5 nemontemi days. Tena adds a sixth nemontemi in the pre-conquest 2 Técpatl year; the post-conquest projection does not extend that intercalation.

(18 × 20) + 5 = 365 days

PROVISIONAL CORRELATION / MODEL OUTPUT

CR-01 · Rafael Tena approach

CR-01 applies Rafael Tena’s reconstruction. Its anchor is 1 Cóatl on 13 August 1521 (Julian), reported for the fall of Tenochtitlan. Historical Julian dates are converted to equivalent Gregorian dates for day counting; modern display dates use the browser’s local Gregorian date.

TENA MODEL ACTIVE · HISTORICAL CORRELATION APPROXIMATE

Tena’s model proposes a sixth nemontemi in selected years. Ivan Šprajc argues against regular intercalation based on the broader evidence. A second commonly cited anchor, Cortés’s entry on 8 November 1519, has a one-day discrepancy against the 1521 anchor.

Validation fixtures: 8 Ehécatl on 8 November 1519, with the duplicated intercalary day accounted for; and 1 Cóatl on 13 August 1521.
